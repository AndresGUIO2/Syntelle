3. Conversation Exception States

Graceful Recovery
Listening allowance exhausted: Microphone listening is paused for today. You can continue conversing by typing text messages (character speech remains available).
Speech allowance exhausted: Daily spoken conversation limit reached. Both text and microphone interaction are paused until access resets in 18 hours 42 minutes.
Microphone access blocked: To speak, enable audio permissions in your browser bar. In the meantime, you can continue typing seamlessly below.
Connection interrupted: Lost contact with the character stream.
